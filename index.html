A Legacy of Faith and Education

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. has been serving the community through Catholic education since 1927.

KCC is a Christ-centered learning community committed to academic excellence, faith formation, and service. It aims to develop students who are knowledgeable, responsible, and guided by Christian values.

  • Providing quality and inclusive Catholic education.
  • Strengthening faith and Christian values.
  • Encouraging meaningful community involvement and service.
